Who is Ali Hassan?

I am Ali Hassan, a highly driven Full Stack Developer and AI Automation Specialist with over 2 years of hands-on experience in architecting and deploying comprehensive digital solutions. On the front lines of development, I specialize in crafting fluid, responsive Web and Mobile Applications. Leveraging modern frameworks like React.js and React Native, I focus on delivering seamless user experiences (UX) and pixel-perfect interfaces that keep users engaged across all devices.

Under the hood, I build the engines that power these applications. With over 50 successfully completed projects, I bring a proven track record of turning complex technical requirements into elegant, automated realities. I am constantly learning, adapting, and pushing the boundaries of what is possible in modern tech.
